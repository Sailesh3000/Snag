## Full description

```
Snag is an AI copilot for job applications. Open it on any application form and it finds the interview-style questions, then drafts answers that sound like you — built from your profile and your own past approved answers.

HOW IT WORKS
• Click the Snag icon on a job application page. Snag detects the form's questions and pre-fills the standard fields (name, email, phone) from your profile.
• For each open-ended question, Snag drafts an answer using your profile plus your previously approved answers as a style guide.
• Review, edit, and approve. Nothing is typed into the form until you approve it.
• When you approve an answer, Snag remembers it locally, so future answers for similar questions get better.

PRIVACY-BY-DESIGN
Your profile, resumes, and answer history are stored only on your own device, in this browser. They are never uploaded to Snag's servers. Answers are generated using your own AI provider account (Anthropic, OpenAI, Groq, or a local Ollama model) directly — that text never touches Snag's servers. The only thing sent to our backend, briefly, is a saved answer's question text, used to generate a similarity embedding for future matching — it is not stored server-side.

FREE
Snag is completely free. Sign in with email to use it — you bring your own AI provider key (or a local Ollama model) to generate answers.

REQUIREMENTS
• Chrome on desktop
• A Snag account (sign in with email)
• Your own Anthropic, OpenAI, or Groq API key — or a local Ollama installation
```
