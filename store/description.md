## Full description

```
Snag is an AI copilot for job applications. Open it on any application form and it finds the interview-style questions, then drafts answers that sound like you — built from your profile and your own past approved answers.

HOW IT WORKS
• Click the Snag icon on a job application page. Snag detects the form's questions and pre-fills the standard fields (name, email, phone) from your profile.
• For each open-ended question, Snag drafts an answer using your profile plus your previously approved answers as a style guide.
• Review, edit, and approve. Nothing is typed into the form until you approve it.
• When you approve an answer, Snag remembers it locally, so future answers for similar questions get better.

PRIVACY-BY-DESIGN
Your profile, resumes, and answer history are stored only on your own device, in this browser. They are never uploaded to Snag's servers. The only data sent to our backend, briefly, is the question text needed to generate an answer — and it is not stored server-side.

SUBSCRIPTION
Snag is a $10/month subscription (billed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record). Answer generation requires an active subscription; you can manage or cancel it any time from your Paddle customer portal. There is no free tier.

REQUIREMENTS
• Chrome on desktop
• A Snag account (sign in with email)
• An active subscription to generate answers
```
